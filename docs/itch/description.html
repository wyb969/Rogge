DASH. BUILD. SURVIVE.

A tiny hero. A growing horde. One more upgrade.

ROGGE is a pixel survival roguelite where your weapons fire automatically and every dash matters. Slip through danger, collect experience, and turn a handful of upgrades into a build that clears the screen.

Free to play in your browser. Keyboard and touch controls supported.

MAKE EVERY RUN YOURS

  • Build around 18 equipment types. Combine automatic weapons, elemental effects, healing, shields, and movement upgrades.
  • Choose your next upgrade. Pick from three cards, spend a reroll, and chase the combination your run needs.
  • Read the fight, then dash. Dodge charging beasts, sword strikes, lightning lanes, and diving guardians.
  • Chase something unexpected. Changing enemy themes, weather, day and night, treasure encounters, and meteor showers add variety to the arena.
  • Find your pace. Easy, Medium, and Hard change the pressure of the horde.

PICK YOUR KIND OF CHAOS

Steel & machines
Axes sweep nearby enemies. Guns pick targets off. A tiny drone adds laser fire.

Spells & elements
Orbiting Books hold a perimeter. Lightning Ring strikes groups. Molotov burns, Frost slows, and Poison Trail turns your escape route into a trap. Reach Poison Trail level 5 to add a slow.

Movement & impact
Wind Emblem turns the end of a dash into a shockwave. Echo Ember rewards chains of kills. Movement Speed, Pickup Range, and directional damage upgrades help shape your approach.

Stay in the fight
Monster Necklace makes defeated enemies drop healing orbs. Add Regeneration, Lifesteal, or Jade Pendant for more ways to recover and protect yourself.

FOUR GUARDIANS. FOUR NEW POWERS.

Push past the charging bull and sword-wielding knight to face four elemental encounters. Defeat a guardian to earn a skill you can activate from the icons on the left of the screen.

  • Tide: restore your health.
  • Thunder: strike enemies across the screen.
  • Wind: gather dropped experience and healing orbs from across the arena. Its two falcons fight at different ranges.
  • Earth & fire: brace behind a powerful defensive barrier.

Strength and Speed Trials put your damage, timing, and dash control to the test between guardian encounters.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD.
  • Dash: Shift. Time it to avoid incoming attacks.
  • Attack: automatic. Focus on movement and your build.
  • Guardian skills: click or tap their icons on the left. You can also use Q to cycle and E to activate the selected skill.
  • Touch: move with the left joystick, then tap DASH or a guardian icon.

ONE MORE RUN?

A treasure guardian can turn a difficult run around with a card-level lottery. A passing meteor shower might clear enemies and leave coins or healing behind. Keep moving. There is always another upgrade to chase.
